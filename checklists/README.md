# Checklists

Ready-to-import master checklists, in the format Admin → Checklists accepts.

To load one: **Admin → Checklists → Upload or paste**, choose the file, name it,
then **Publish** and give it a schedule.

| File | Built from | Items |
| --- | --- | --- |
| `dir-of-ops-snapshot.csv` | The Dir of Ops Snapshot Report exported from the previous platform | 46 across 8 sections; 34 scored |

## Notes on the Dir of Ops Snapshot

Scoring matches the old platform exactly: the 34 Yes/No questions carry equal
weight, and photos and free-text answers are not scored. A visit with two
answers of "No" scores 94.12, the same figure the exported report showed.

Deliberately not carried over, because the app records them itself: store
number, final score, and the inspector's email address.
